# Puppet Fight CPC — Character Roster

Characters are based on the puppets of ***Puppet Regime***, GZERO Media's
(Eurasia Group) felt-puppet political satire. It began in 2017, uses ~90-second
scripted sketches, and is voiced mostly by Alex Kliment. The show's puppets
are felt-and-foam hand puppets with "eight fingers and no legs". Our
fighters get legs so they can kick.

Status key: ✅ done · 🎯 next wave · 📋 later · ❓ unconfirmed in the show

## Current *Puppet Regime* cast

These are the official show billings from GZERO's "About the Show" page.

### Donald Trump ✅ — "The Greatest American 2.0"
- **Show persona**: back in office and ready to claim and rename whatever he
  wants. Tagline "Deals! Deals! Deals!". Scat-jazz voice cadence.
- **Look**: yellow swoop of hair, orange face, pursed mouth, navy suit, very
  long red tie, small hands.
- **Specials**: **Tweet Storm** ✅ (phone throw, tweet-bird projectile).
  Planned: **The Art of the Deal** (handshake grab that yanks the opponent
  in), **Rename It!** (taunt that renames the opponent on the HUD and briefly stuns them).
- **Victory**: double thumbs-up shimmy ✅. Taunt: "Tremendous. Nobody fights better."

### Vladimir Putin ✅ — "Meddler-in-Chief"
- **Show persona**: "America's most persistent troll", giving ruthless advice
  on his own public-access show. Self-styled wellness expert ("This
  Authoritarian Life"). "Feline malice". Recent sketches show him shirtless
  with an Orthodox cross and an ushanka, dancing to EDM.
- **Look**: pale egg-shaped head, receding light hair, narrow eyes, smirk,
  a white sleeveless vest over bare arms (the strongman image the show plays
  on), grey trousers.
- **Specials**: **Judo Throw** ✅ (grab, hip load, throw). Planned:
  **Shirtless Bear Rush** (ushanka on, shirt off, charging shoulder).
  Alternate costume: shirtless with ushanka.
- **Victory**: strongman flex ✅ (needs re-posing so it reads in profile).

### Kim Jong-Un 🎯 — "The Supremest Leader"
- **Show persona**: a finely coiffed, short-tempered despot with a taste for
  surf rock, edibles and Cardi B. The 2018 sketch "Trump and Kim Enter the
  Ring" already framed the summit as a heavyweight showdown, which makes
  him the natural third fighter.
- **Look**: tall flat-top haircut with shaved sides, round face, dark Mao suit,
  big glasses optional.
- **Specials**: **Surf's Up Missile** (rides a missile-surfboard across the
  stage), **Button Slam** (giant red button: ground shockwave). Gag
  rivalry with Trump: "Mine is bigger."
- **Palette**: black hair (outline pen + navy shading), pink skin, dark grey or navy suit.

### Xi Jinping 🎯 — "President Emperor of the People's Republic of China"
- **Show persona**: "the big daddy of China", known for a friendship
  "with(out) limits" (with Putin). Thinks about Taiwan. Sketches include a
  boy-band number with Putin and Trump, and a game show against Biden.
- **Look**: broad smooth face, neat black side-parted hair, dark suit, red tie.
- **Specials**: **Friendship Without Limits** (hug-throw that won't let go),
  **Belt and Road** (belt whip with long horizontal reach).
- **Palette**: shares the suit and red-tie ramps with Trump. Skin is pastel yellow → olive.

### Elon Musk 🎯 — "Tech Support"
- **Show persona**: the richest man in the world and a big fan of chainsaws.
- **Look**: tall, black T-shirt, dark hair, big grin.
- **Specials**: **Chainsaw Rampage** (a slapstick toy chainsaw, sparks only,
  no gore), **Rocket Uppercut** (anti-air that launches on a little rocket).
- **Palette**: black T-shirt (outline + navy), pink skin. The chainsaw is grey with a red accent.

### Volodymyr Zelensky 🎯 — "Humble President of Ukraine"
- **Show persona**: a freedom fighter who could always use more ammo, a
  running joke in the show.
- **Look**: olive-green military T-shirt or fleece, short beard, close-cropped hair.
- **Specials**: **More Ammo!** (calls in a supply crate that drops on the
  opponent), **Stand-Up Comic** (taunt, a nod to his comedy career, that
  stuns briefly).
- **Palette**: olive (808000) outfit ramp. Pink skin with dark red beard shading.
- **Tone note**: keep it to the show's gags (supplies, determination). No
  war imagery.

### Narendra Modi 📋 — "Saffron Minister of India"
- **Show persona**: an aspiring nationalist strongman who is also a
  Bollywood crooner heartthrob.
- **Look**: white beard and hair, glasses, saffron/orange kurta with a waistcoat.
- **Specials**: **Bollywood Spin** (a dance-number spinning kick), **Crooner**
  (song notes as a slow wave projectile).
- **Palette**: saffron (FF8000) outfit, white beard. Skin uses orange → dark red,
  so it must be kept distinct from Trump's (more dark red, less highlight).

### Mohammed bin Salman 📋 — "The Crown Prince of Darkness"
- **Show persona**: a regular guy who tells jokes, makes roasts, and "orders hits".
- **Look**: red-and-white checked shemagh, black beard, white thobe.
- **Specials**: **Oil Slick** (floor hazard), **Royal Roast** (insult taunt
  projectile). Keep it absurd. No references to real killings.
- **Palette**: white thobe, red/white headdress (bright red + white), black beard.

## Past and occasional puppets

Seen in earlier episodes: 2020-2022 sketches and GZERO's "2021: The Year in
Puppet Regime".

### Joe Biden 📋
- **Show persona**: "chronic anachronism", "Trillion Dollar Joe", struggled to
  pick a Halloween costume and to pardon the Thanksgiving turkey, "Dancin'
  for Manchin". Game show against Xi.
- **Specials**: **Aviator Glide** (aviator shades on, slow dash), **Trillion
  Dollar Rain** (money shower projectile). Victory: eats an ice cream.

### Emmanuel Macron 📋
- **Show persona**: "debilitating arrogance". Squabbled with the UK over submarines.
- **Specials**: **Jupiter Strike** (a grand, slow overhead), **Le Shrug**
  (taunt that deflects one projectile).

### Angela Merkel 📋
- **Show persona**: eager to exit after 16 years. Vaccine "drug deal of the
  century". Farewell record was a Kraftwerk remix.
- **Specials**: **Merkel Rhombus** (her signature hand pose as a shield
  block), **Autobahn** (dash, Kraftwerk-style).
- **Look**: blonde bob, coloured blazer (a pastel or bright pen), black trousers.

### Jair Bolsonaro 📋 and Lula 📋 — "Brazil's own Godzilla vs Kong"
- **Show persona**: Bolsonaro dreading a clash with his old nemesis Lula,
  framed as Godzilla vs Kong. They work as a rivalry pair, with a special
  intro when they meet.
- **Specials (Bolsonaro)**: **Godzilla Stomp** (ground pound).
  **(Lula)**: **Kong Smash** (double-fist overhead).

### Mark Zuckerberg 📋
- **Show persona**: "android megalomania".
- **Specials**: **Metaverse Glitch** (teleport), **Data Harvest** (grab that
  drains a little health back to him). Victory: robotic sunscreen-faced surf.

### Ian Bremmer 📋 — "The Puppet Master" (secret boss)
- The show's very first puppet: GZERO founder Ian Bremmer's puppet started
  as a gag gift. A fitting final boss for the arcade ladder.
- **Specials**: **G-Zero World** (screen-wide "no leader in charge" stun),
  **Puppet Strings** (pulls the opponent across the screen).

### Boris Johnson ❓
- GZERO's 2021 recap mentions UK-France squabbling, but we couldn't confirm a
  Johnson puppet. Check the show archive before modelling him.

## Character checklist (per new fighter)

1. Write the Blender spec + head function in `tools/blender_puppets.py`
   (proportions, materials, caricature features).
2. Choose 4 personal colours that fit the shared-palette scheme
   ([GAME_PLAN.md §3.3](GAME_PLAN.md#33-palette-strategy)).
3. Pose the character-specific `special` and `victory` moves. The common
   moves are inherited from the base set.
4. Render → `make_sprites.py` → Aseprite touch-up → `cpc_export.py`.
5. Fill in move data (damage, frame timings, hitboxes) and the select-screen title.
6. Check the 32K size budget.
